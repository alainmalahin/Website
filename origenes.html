ERA I · ORIGINS

Before cities.
Before laws.
Before written words.

There was a time when human beings challenged the deep darkness of caves to imprint their souls upon stone.

With traces of fire, charcoal and ochre, our ancestors painted humanity's first declaration of existence.

Art was not born to decorate.

It was born as a bridge between human beings, nature and the invisible.

ERA · ORIGINS was born as a tribute to that first creative impulse.

It does not seek to recreate the past.

It seeks to remind us that every story has an origin.

Each design in this collection is inspired by the earliest artistic expressions of humanity.

This is the first story of ALAIN MALAHIN.
