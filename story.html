MY STORY

My name is Alain Malahin.

ALAIN MALAHIN was born from a childhood dream.

Since I was young, I was fascinated by clothing—not because of fashion trends, but because I wanted to wear something unique.

I often imagined garments unlike anything I could find and even asked my mother to help bring some of those ideas to life.

Life eventually led me to spend more than twenty years working in hospitality.

BUT THAT DREAM NEVER DISAPPEARED.

HOW WAS
ALAIN MALAHIN BORN?

Everything changed during a journey to Crete in 2026.

For the first time in many years, I had the opportunity to stop, reflect and reconnect with the dream that had accompanied me since childhood.

I realised that this project could no longer remain only an idea.

I did not have every resource.

BUT I HAD THE DETERMINATION TO BEGIN.

I did not want to create just another fashion brand.

I wanted to build something meaningful.

A brand where every collection tells a story.

A BRAND INSPIRED BY HISTORY, CULTURE AND IDENTITY.

WHY DOES
ALAIN MALAHIN EXIST?

History has always fascinated me.

The cultures, symbols and identities created by humanity across thousands of years.

I wanted to transform that inspiration into design.

Not simply clothing.
