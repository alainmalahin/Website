HISTORY OF
ALAIN MALAHIN

Every story has an origin.
This is mine.

WHY DOES ALAIN MALAHIN EXIST?

ALAIN MALAHIN was born from a childhood dream. Since I was young, I was fascinated by clothing—not because of fashion trends, but because I wanted to wear something unique. I often imagined garments unlike anything I could find and even asked my mother to help bring some of those ideas to life. Life eventually led me to spend more than twenty years working in hospitality, but that dream never disappeared. It remained quietly with me, waiting for the right moment.

HOW WAS ALAIN MALAHIN BORN?

Everything changed during a journey to Crete. For the first time in many years, I had the opportunity to stop, reflect and reconnect with the dream that had accompanied me since childhood. I realised that I didn't want to create just another fashion brand. I wanted to build something meaningful. A brand where every collection tells a story. A brand inspired by history, culture and identity.

WHY ERA?

Fashion changes constantly. History remains forever. That is why every ALAIN MALAHIN collection belongs to an ERA. Each launch is inspired by a civilisation, a culture or a defining moment in human history. We do not simply create clothing. We create stories you can wear.

WHY DOES ALAIN MALAHIN EXIST?

As a child I imagined unique clothes and even asked my mother to make garments inspired by my own ideas. WHY ERA?

Modern fashion often follows temporary trends. I wanted something different. Every collection represents a different civilization, culture or moment in human history. Each launch tells a story. Each design belongs to an ERA. ALAIN MALAHIN is more than clothing. It is history. It is identity. It is YOUR ERA.
