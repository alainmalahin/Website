HISTORY OF
ALAIN MALAHIN

Every story has an origin.
This is mine.

01

WHY DOES
ALAIN MALAHIN EXIST?

ALAIN MALAHIN was born from an idea that had been with me since childhood.

I was always drawn to clothing, not because I wanted to follow fashion, but because I wanted to wear something different.

I imagined garments that did not yet exist and even asked my mother to transform some of those ideas into clothes.

Life eventually led me to spend more than twenty years working in hospitality.

But the idea never disappeared.

02

HOW WAS
ALAIN MALAHIN BORN?

Everything changed during a journey to Crete in 2026.

There, I found the time to reflect and understood that the moment had come to begin a new path.

This project could no longer remain only an idea.

I did not have every resource.

But I had the determination to begin.

I did not want to create just another fashion brand.

I wanted to unite history, design and identity.

03

WHY ERA?

Fashion changes.

History remains.

Every ALAIN MALAHIN collection represents a different moment in human history.

A civilisation. A culture. An origin.

Each collection has its own identity.

Each design belongs to an ERA.

We do not simply create clothing.

We create stories you can wear.

Every story has an origin.
