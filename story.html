MY STORY

My name is Alain Malahin.

For many years, fashion was an idea that lived quietly in my mind.

Life took me through different countries, cultures and experiences.

I worked. I travelled. I observed.

But the idea never disappeared.

HOW WAS
ALAIN MALAHIN BORN?

Everything changed during a journey to Crete in 2026.

There, I found the time to reflect and understood that the moment had come to begin a new path.

This project could no longer remain only an idea.

I did not have every resource.

But I had the determination to begin.

I did not want to create just another fashion brand.

I wanted to unite history, design and identity.
