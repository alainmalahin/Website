HISTORY OF
ALAIN MALAHIN

Every story has an origin.
This is mine.

MY STORY

ALAIN MALAHIN was born from a curiosity that had been with me since I was a child. I was always drawn to clothing, not because I wanted to follow fashion, but because I wanted to wear something different.

I imagined different garments and even asked my mother to knit clothes inspired by my own ideas.

As time passed, I continued to feel an interest in fashion, although for many years I thought it was a dream too distant or even impossible.

HOW IT BEGAN

During a trip to Crete in June 2026 and after more than twenty years working in hospitality, I understood that the time had come to begin a new path.

On this journey I found time to reflect and understood that this project should not remain just an idea.

I did not have all the resources, but I had the determination to begin.

AND NOT AS JUST ANOTHER BRAND.

But as a project that seeks to unite history, design and identity in every collection.

WHY ERA?

I understood that the fashion market is full of collections that follow passing trends.

I wanted to create something different.

Not just clothing.

I WANTED TO CREATE COLLECTIONS WITH MEANING.
