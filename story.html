HISTORY OF
ALAIN MALAHIN

Every story has an origin.
This is mine.

WHY DOES ALAIN MALAHIN EXIST?

ALAIN MALAHIN was born from a feeling that has been with me since childhood. Fashion never attracted me because it was trendy. It attracted me because I wanted to wear something different. As a child I imagined unique clothes and even asked my mother to make garments inspired by my own ideas. Although life led me to spend more than twenty years working in hospitality, that dream never disappeared.

HOW WAS ALAIN MALAHIN BORN?

Everything changed during a journey to Crete. For the first time in years I found the time to stop, reflect and understand that my project could no longer remain just an idea. I didn't have every resource. But I had determination. I didn't want to build just another fashion brand. I wanted to create a universe where history, design and identity become one.

WHY ERA?

Modern fashion often follows temporary trends. I wanted something different. Every collection represents a different civilization, culture or moment in human history. Each launch tells a story. Each design belongs to an ERA. ALAIN MALAHIN is more than clothing. It is history. It is identity. It is YOUR ERA.
